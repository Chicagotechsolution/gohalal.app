How it works

Open the app, see what's actually zabiha around you.

Search your area

Restaurants, groceries, and butchers appear on one map, filtered by what matters to you: hand-slaughtered only, specific certifiers, or no cross-contamination.

Read the sourcing card

Every listing carries a sourcing card: supplier, slaughter method per protein, certification, and kitchen handling. No vague "halal available" labels.

Save your spots

Keep a personal list for home, work, and travel. When a listing's sourcing changes, the card updates and your saved copy shows the new date.

Why sourcing cards

"Halal" on the door isn't a standard. A sourcing card is.

Two restaurants with the same halal sign can source completely differently — one hand-slaughtered and certified, the other machine-processed with no paperwork. GoHalal records the answers a careful eater would ask for at the counter and publishes them on the listing.

You apply your own standard. The app gives you the facts to apply it with.
